Our story

The same old comal

Eulalia learned to make tortillas standing on a wooden crate beside her grandmother's comal. Years later she opened this little kitchen with that same comal, a handful of recipes, and her two kids folding napkins after school.

Those kids run the place now. The masa is still ground every morning, the mole still takes two days, and Eulalia still comes by to taste it and tell us what it needs.
